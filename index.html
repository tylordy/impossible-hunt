    How to play
    Cameras on poles sweep a beam across the streets. Step into the beam and it turns red and the alert jumps.
    Police (dark blue, with caps) chase you and hit you when they catch up. Stun them with a baton or taser.
    Everyone else (postmen, teachers, nurses, workers, joggers) runs and reports where they saw you.
    Glowing crates hold a baton, bat, slingshot, taser charges, smoke bombs, flashbangs, caltrops and medkits.
    Lit doorways (shops, cafés, lobbies, metro) lead inside. Cameras can't see you indoors, and the metro has three exits across the city. Police right behind you can follow you in.
    Wheels and rails: take any car or scooter with E. Police cars patrol and will ram you. Four train stops (blue T on the minimap, tall blue beams in the sky, and an arrow at the top of the screen pointing to the nearest) sit at street level by the elevated rail. Stand on the glowing blue pad, wait for a train to stop, then press E to board.
